# Improve Your Akhirah — Project Brief

A complete, free Islamic companion website: Quran, Hadith, translations, audio, daily tools and user accounts, under its own name, logo and design. Islam360 is the reference for scope, but no content, design or branding is copied from it or from any other app.

## Scope (all on free services)

- **Complete Quran:** all 114 surahs in Arabic, several Urdu and English translations, word-by-word meaning, transliteration, tafsir, and a printed-Mushaf page view.
- **Quran audio:** many well-known reciters, verse-by-verse playback with highlighting, and a repeat option for memorisation.
- **Hadith:** Sahih Bukhari, Sahih Muslim, Abu Dawud, Tirmidhi, Nasai, Ibn Majah and more, in Arabic, Urdu and English, with gradings where available.
- **Search:** one search box across the Quran and Hadith in Arabic, Urdu, English and Roman Urdu.
- **Daily tools:** prayer times, Qibla direction, Hijri calendar, Tasbeeh counter, Duas and Azkar, 99 Names of Allah, Zakat calculator.
- **User accounts:** sign up with email or Google; bookmarks, personal notes, reading progress and a Khatam tracker, synced across devices.
- **Three languages:** Arabic, English and Urdu, with proper right-to-left layout.
- **Mobile:** installable as a PWA; saved surahs readable offline.
- **Own brand:** new name, logo and design.

## Constraints

- **No copying from Islam360 or other apps.** Use the original, free, openly published sources they rely on.
- **Prophet and Sahaba stories** have no free ready-made dataset. Write them from Quran and Hadith references; a scholar or knowledgeable person must review them before they go live.
- **Translation licences vary.** Check each translation's terms and use only those that allow it.
- **Free hosting tiers are often non-commercial only.** If the site will earn money (ads, donations to a business, etc.), pick a host/plan that allows it before launch. Development and demos stay free either way.
- **Qibla compass** may not work in every phone browser; show the Qibla direction on a map as a fallback.
- **Free service limits** are fine for launch and early growth; some may need paid plans at scale.
